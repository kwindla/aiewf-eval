# Balanced history-origin crossed-prefix follow-up

Date frozen: 2026-08-08, before any follow-up inference output was collected.

## Motivation and scope

The completed 12-prefix replay found a precise conditional BF16-minus-FP8
effect at turn 12, but a post-hoc split reversed sign by prefix provenance.
That bank contained four local BF16 histories, four local FP8 histories, and
four BaseTen histories. It was not designed to estimate a history-origin by
inference-configuration interaction.

This follow-up uses the existing 150 local-BF16 and 150 local-FP8 on-policy
conversations. It does not collect more full conversations. It freezes the
turn-12 state from every prerequisite-valid history and crosses every frozen
state with both matched local KV configurations. The study measures task and
tool-use behavior on this benchmark, not general intelligence.

## Frozen corpus

- Target: scripted turn 12 only.
- Sources: all 150 canonical local-BF16 conversations and all 150 canonical
  local-FP8 conversations from the N=30 discovery campaigns plus N=120
  extensions.
- Eligibility is evaluated without using the turn-12 response: scripted turns
  0 through 12 must be present, Jennifer Smith must occur in the prefix, and
  turn 11 must contain a successful `submit_session_suggestion` call for
  Jennifer Smith. If the eligible counts differ, the larger stratum is reduced
  to the smaller count by lowest salted SHA-256 selection hash. If they are
  equal, all eligible histories are used.
- The source turn-12 outcome and judgment are never inputs to eligibility,
  selection, snapshot construction, or seed allocation.
- Each snapshot preserves the exact logged OpenAI-compatible message context
  and original tools/sampling request. Generated call IDs are not rewritten.
- Snapshot and source transcript/log hashes are frozen in the manifest.

The corpus is a balanced sample of histories generated by the two production
paths. Crossing those histories with inference dtype makes one cell on-policy
and the other off-policy within each origin. The interaction is therefore an
effect-modification result over observed histories; it does not, by itself,
prove an abstract "manifold compatibility" mechanism.

## Arms and serving controls

The two inference arms reuse the audited server and scorer from the parent
study:

1. NVFP4 weights plus BF16 KV.
2. The same NVFP4 weights plus unit-scaled FP8 E4M3 KV.

Both use pinned SGLang, compact 16,000-token geometry, SWA/full ratio 0.35,
Triton attention, PyTorch seeded sampling shim, CUDA graphs disabled,
concurrency one, temperature 1.0, top-p 0.95, no top-k field, warm
RadixAttention, and a 512-token output cap. The parent study established cap
parity and seeded repeatability. Frozen prompt token IDs must match across
arms for all snapshots before collection.

## Seed allocation and order

Each history receives 16 deterministic pseudo-random nonnegative seeds derived
from SHA-256 of the study version, snapshot ID, and within-history replicate.
Seeds are unique across histories and paired between inference arms. This
avoids treating repeated evaluations of a small history bank as independent
trajectories and avoids a shared numeric-seed cluster across all histories.

Collection has four macro blocks to balance time/order drift:

1. FP8, within-history replicates 0-7.
2. BF16, replicates 0-7.
3. BF16, replicates 8-15.
4. FP8, replicates 8-15.

Every block has an immutable request plan bound to its live container, frozen
snapshot manifest, token manifest, seed manifest, request bodies, and source
hashes. Collection is serial. A snapshot prefix is primed immediately before
its eight measured requests; every measured request must report at least 90%
cached prompt tokens. Request, parser, truncation, provenance, allocation, or
cache-gate failure stops the block.

## Outcomes and estimands

The frozen mechanical scorer from the parent study is authoritative. Success
means the correct `submit_session_suggestion` tool call with Jennifer Smith and
the state-machine/complex-workflows suggestion. The same frozen error
categories are secondary outcomes.

For each history, compute the paired mean BF16-minus-FP8 success difference
over its 16 allocated seeds. Primary estimands, on the percentage-point scale,
are:

1. The equal-history-weighted direct KV effect among local-BF16-origin
   histories.
2. The corresponding effect among local-FP8-origin histories.
3. The history-origin interaction: estimand 1 minus estimand 2.

The equal-weighted 50/50 origin-mixture effect is secondary. On-policy diagonal
success (BF16 inference on BF16-origin history versus FP8 inference on
FP8-origin history) is descriptive only because it compares different frozen
states and is not paired by trajectory.

## Inference and decision rules

Histories, not replay rows, are the independent sampling units. Report
history-cluster bootstrap 95% percentile intervals using 100,000 deterministic
replicates, resampling histories independently within each origin and keeping
all 16 paired seed outcomes together. Also report:

- a two-stage sensitivity bootstrap that resamples histories and paired seeds
  within histories;
- per-history effects and their empirical distribution;
- the fraction of histories favoring each arm or tying;
- outcome-category tables by origin and inference arm;
- cohort (N=30 versus N=120) sensitivity estimates.

The three primary intervals are descriptive family members; Holm-adjusted
randomization p-values are not claimed because history origin was not randomly
assigned. Evidence for a practically important compatibility interaction
requires the 95% history-cluster interval for estimand 3 to exclude zero and
have absolute magnitude at least 5 percentage points. Otherwise report the
interval as the bound supplied by this study rather than declaring
equivalence. No optional stopping is used: all 16 seeds for all frozen
histories are collected unless a fail-closed gate stops collection.

## Interpretation boundary

This study can determine whether the instantaneous BF16-versus-FP8 tool-call
effect depends on whether a turn-12 history was produced by the BF16 or FP8
path. It cannot separate every textual property that differs between those
histories, estimate a general intelligence construct, or replace the existing
on-policy N=150-per-arm whole-conversation estimate. More on-policy
conversations are warranted only if a narrower production-level total-effect
interval is still required after this crossed-prefix interaction is known.
